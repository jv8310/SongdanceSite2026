# Somatic Rising

## The right practice, in the right moment, in your language.

**Investor Memorandum — June 2026 (Seed Round)**

---

Somatic Rising is an AI companion for deep personal healing. It reads where you are in your development, speaks to you in the language that reaches you, and composes — live, in the moment — the practice you need right now: a guided journey, an inner-child dialogue, a sounding session, set to music generated for you. Not a library you browse. A companion that knows you, and gets to know you better every week.

We are raising **€900K in seed capital** (range €850K–1M, convertible instruments plus a parallel grant track) to bring the first version to market in English-speaking Western markets and prove, with real retention data, that adaptive AI-composed healing outperforms the content libraries that dominate this category.

## The Moment

Three forces are converging, and none of the incumbents is positioned at their intersection.

**The demand is structural, not cyclical.** One in eight people globally lives with a mental health condition, and more than half of affected adults in the US receive no treatment at all. The US Surgeon General has declared loneliness a public health epidemic. Beneath the clinical numbers sits something deeper: a meaning crisis. Gen Z reports poor *spiritual* health at three times the rate of baby boomers, and 66% of US adults now describe a personal commitment to spiritual practice — most of them outside any institution. Millions of people are not looking for symptom management. They are looking for transformation, and they are assembling it themselves.

**The behavior already exists.** People are already using general-purpose AI chatbots as confidants and de facto counselors, in enormous numbers. The demand for a wise, always-available conversational presence is proven. What those tools cannot do is *take you through* anything — they talk, but they cannot guide a practice, hold a process, or remember your inner landscape with any structure. And they draw on the open internet: a mixture of genuine wisdom and noise.

**The technology just crossed the threshold.** Generative AI can now sustain a nuanced, emotionally attuned conversation and compose voice and music in real time. Somatic and body-based healing has gone mainstream. The capability and the cultural readiness arrived at the same time. The window is open now.

## The Product

The core of Somatic Rising is a single loop, executed with depth:

**Read → Meet → Compose → Guide → Learn → Deepen.**

The companion converses with you and builds a structured picture of where you stand — using developmental meta-frameworks (Integral stage models and the Enneagram at launch) inferred gently through conversation rather than extracted through a quiz. It then meets you *there*: the tone, the pacing, the depth, even the kind of practice it offers are calibrated to your stage and type. The same grief is met differently in different people — that is the difference between a library and a companion.

When the moment calls for more than conversation, the companion composes a practice on the spot — script, voice, and music generated for this person, this moment. The MVP launches with three signature modalities, chosen because generative composition transforms them rather than merely delivering them:

**Generative guided journeys.** Visualizations and meditations built around your actual situation, your imagery, your pace — with music matched to the arc of the session. The clearest, fastest "this was made for me" experience.

**Inner-child and constellation work.** Guided imaginal dialogue — with your younger self, with family, with people present or passed. Inherently personal, emotionally profound, and entirely absent from the app landscape. This is our depth differentiator.

**Somatic sounding and authentic voice.** Guided vocal release and embodied sound practice. This is what makes the product *somatic* rather than another eyes-closed meditation app — active, in the body, and distinctly ours.

**Safety is a launch feature, not a roadmap item.** A system that guides people into grief and inner-child territory carries real responsibility. The companion is designed to dose and pace — to know when *not* to go deeper, when to ground, and when to point a person toward human support. We position deliberately outside regulated clinical care: this is healing and personal transformation, not medical treatment, and the product's language, claims, and escalation pathways are designed accordingly. Trust is part of the moat.

## The Moat: Knowledge That Compounds

Features can be copied. What cannot be copied quickly is *accumulated knowing*.

**Compounding personal context.** Every conversation and every practice deepens a structured, living model of the user — stage, type, history, what has landed, what they are moving through. Month six is dramatically better than month one *for that specific person*, and none of it transfers to a competitor. This is a switching cost that grows with time. The market evidence is suggestive: Replika, with far shallower memory and no developmental model, sustains 7+ month average paid retention in a category where 30-day retention averages 3%.

**A curated, licensed knowledge base.** Where general AI draws on the open internet, our companion draws on structured knowledge from the best teachers and authors in each lineage — licensed, with consent, and attributed. Usage is tracked at the source level from day one, enabling a **creator royalty pool**: a defined share of subscription revenue (modeled at ~25%) divided among knowledge partners by actual usage, Spotify-style. This solves the creator economy gap in wellness, gives revered teachers a reason to bring their work to *us* exclusively, and builds a supply-side flywheel competitors must negotiate lineage by lineage to match.

**Speed as strategy.** Because the moat is time-based — accumulated user context, accumulated licensed knowledge — every month in market compounds. We ship a thin version of the full loop early and let real users deepen it.

## Business Model & Unit Economics

**Subscription, premium D2C.** Modeled at €19/month with a discounted annual plan; roughly three quarters of revenue to the company, one quarter to the creator royalty pool at scale.

The model targets the investor-standard **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1**, and we are explicit about the path: the category's economics live or die on retention, and retention is precisely what compounding personal context is engineered to drive. Benchmarks: top wellness subscriptions hold churn under 3% monthly against a 5.3% category average; at €19/month, sub-4% churn and a blended CAC of €60–90 clears 3:1 with room. Annual plans (which cut churn by half in category data), conversion optimization, and community-led acquisition are the supporting levers. Proving these numbers with live cohorts is the central purpose of this round.

Later phases add a second engine: a **practitioner layer** — on-demand human facilitators, AI-prepared and context-briefed — taking a marketplace margin. It is scoped, researched, and deliberately deferred.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Prove the loop (months 0–9).** Public launch of the MVP: conversational companion, Integral + Enneagram profiling inferred in conversation, the three signature modalities, the memory layer, and the safety architecture. Royalty *tracking* built in from day one; payouts activate when revenue justifies them.

**Phase 2 — Deepen and widen (months 9–18).** Expand the profiling stack (astrology, Human Design, numerology as optional lenses), grow the licensed knowledge base and activate the creator pool, add modalities, integrate wearable signals where they genuinely improve practice selection.

**Phase 3 — The human layer (post-Series A).** On-demand practitioner matching with AI session support; physical and corporate experiences under the Somatic Rising brand. The long-term vision is an ecosystem where AI companionship and human facilitation reinforce each other.
