# Funding Patterns & Unit Economics: Wellness/Mental Health Tech Startups
## Research Briefing — February 2026

---

## 1. Funding Landscape Overview (2022–2025)

Mental health startups raised **$1.06B in 2023** and **$1.13B in 2024** (a 23% YoY increase), with the first half of 2025 alone reaching ~$1.32B. Combined 2024–2025 funding of ~$2.45B represents the highest two-year period for mental health startup investment, surpassing the previous 2021–2022 record of $1.8B.

Key dynamics:
- Average deal size grew from **$5.3M (2023)** to **$6.5M (2024)**, indicating investor preference for proven models over speculative bets
- Seed and Series A rounds comprised **45% of deal volume** but only **18% of total funding value** — capital is concentrating in growth-stage companies
- AI applications (chatbots, voice biomarkers, predictive risk modeling) dominate funding allocation
- Mental health & wellness received **$1.1B in angel investments** specifically in 2023
- Broader wellness startups raised nearly **$7B in 2024** across all stages

## 2. Typical Seed Round Sizes

### Pre-Seed
- **Range:** $100K–$500K (most common), up to $1M for strong teams
- **Median US pre-seed (2024):** ~$500K–$750K on Carta
- **Median valuation cap:** ~$5M (dipped ~10% in Q1 2024 from a prior plateau)
- Best practice: keep pre-seed under $500K to maintain a clean cap table

### Seed
- **Median US seed round (2024):** $2.5M (Carta data)
- **Typical range for wellness/mental health:** $1M–$5M
- **Equity reserved:** Typically 10–20% for seed round
- Notable wellness seed examples:
  - **Inner Cosmos** (neurostimulation for depression): $10M seed (2022)
  - **CredibleMind** (mental wellness platform): $7.5M Series A (2024)
  - **Little Otter** (children's mental health): $9.5M pre-series (2024)
  - **BeMe** (peer support): $12M Series A with 85% retention after 6 months
  - **Slingshot AI / Ash** (AI therapy): $93M total — one of the best-funded newer entrants
  - **Woebot Health** early: raised first funding on clinical CBT chatbot premise, grew to $124M total
  - **Wysa** early rounds: $30.5M over 10 rounds starting from seed scale

## 3. Funding Structures

### Dominant Instrument: SAFEs
- **83% of pre-seed investments** on Carta in H1 2023 were SAFEs
- **64% of all seed deals** (Q3 2023–Q3 2024) used SAFEs
- **92% of pre-seed rounds** used SAFEs in 2024 (up from 54% in 2019)
- Standard Y Combinator SAFE terms with valuation cap and/or discount

### Convertible Notes
- Now just **9–10% of seed deals** (declining rapidly)
- More common in **medical device and hardware** sectors where customizability matters
- Still occasionally preferred by European investors and in health-adjacent deals with regulatory pathways (FDA/CE marking)
- Include interest rates and maturity dates (unlike SAFEs)

### Priced Equity Rounds
- **~27% of seed deals** in the year ending Q3 2024
- More common for serial entrepreneurs or companies with existing traction
- More typical from Series A onward in mental health

### Structure Recommendation for AI Wellness Startups
Pre-seed via SAFE notes (with valuation cap) is the current norm. Transition to a priced round at Series A when you have product-market fit data and meaningful traction metrics. In Europe, convertible notes remain more common than SAFEs but the gap is closing.

**Best fit for wellness apps:** Revenue-based financing works once you have 6+ months of subscription revenue at $10K+/month. It's ideal for funding marketing spend and growth without dilution, complementing equity rounds.

**Typical range for AI wellness apps:** $10–$20/month or $60–$100/year. Premium/holistic positioning can support $15–$30/month ($150–$300/year) for the "wellness-seeking, therapy-curious" demographic.

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

- **General app CAC:** $50–$150 per acquired customer (industry benchmark)
- **Health & fitness apps:** Estimated $30–$80 for organic-heavy acquisition; $80–$150+ for paid channels
- **BetterHelp** spends $8M+/month on podcast ads alone — reflects extremely high CAC in therapy space; 80% of sign-ups never become active users
- **Calm & Headspace:** ~7% 14-day retention of new downloads, suggesting roughly 7% of installs convert to paying
- **Subscription CAC average (cross-category):** $72 (Marketing LTB benchmark)
- **Freemium model** (Headspace-style) shown to improve CAC efficiency vs. free trial model

### Churn Rates

| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| **Average subscription churn (cross-category)** | 5.3% monthly |
| **Top-performing subscriptions** | <3% monthly churn |
| **Health & fitness app 30-day retention** | 3% average; 8–12% for strong performers; top apps reach 25–47.5% |
| **Calm 30-day retention** | ~8.3% (Apptopia) |
| **Headspace 30-day retention** | ~7.7% (Apptopia) |
| **Insight Timer 30-day retention** | ~16% (higher engagement, community-driven) |
| **Annual subscription retention (H&F)** | ~33% |
| **Annual plans vs. monthly** | Reduce churn by 51%; 2.4x more profitable |
| **Meditation apps** | Lowest churn among wellness categories |
| **12-month subscriber retention (industry)** | Dropped ~14% in latest year across categories |
| **Reactivation rate** | 10%+ of churned monthly subs re-subscribe within 12 months |

### Lifetime Value (LTV) & LTV:CAC

- **Target LTV:CAC ratio:** ≥3:1 for sustainable growth (industry standard)
- **Headspace reported:** LTV/CAC >3:1 (strong unit economics)
- **Illustrative model for a $15/mo wellness app:**
  - At 4% monthly churn → average customer lifetime = 25 months
  - LTV = $15 × 25 × ~90% margin = ~$337
  - If CAC = $80 → LTV:CAC = 4.2:1 (healthy)
  - If CAC = $150 → LTV:CAC = 2.2:1 (needs optimization)
- **CAC payback period target:** Under 12 months
- **Key lever:** Conversion from install → paid is the single biggest growth lever; even small improvements to onboarding and paywall copy significantly impact LTV:CAC

## 7. Investor Expectations for Seed-Stage Wellness Apps (2025–2026)

Based on current market dynamics, investors at seed stage are looking for:

1. **Proven unit economics** — even early evidence of LTV:CAC ≥3:1
2. **Insurance reimbursement potential** or clear B2B/enterprise path (major trend)
3. **Clinical outcomes data** — real metrics, not just claims
4. **Sticky usage** — retention significantly above the 3% 30-day average
5. **AI differentiation** — proprietary models, personalization engines, or novel therapeutic approaches
6. **Clear regulatory awareness** — understanding FDA/CE/GDPR implications
7. **Capital efficiency** — smaller teams generating meaningful traction

**What's less fundable right now:** Pure content libraries without AI personalization, D2C-only models without enterprise/payer strategy, "me-too" meditation apps, and teams without domain expertise in both therapy/wellness AND technology.

## 8. Strategic Funding Pathway for an AI Wellness/Somatic App

### Recommended Phased Approach

**Phase 1: Pre-Seed ($150K–$500K)**
- Instrument: SAFE notes with $5–8M valuation cap
- Sources: Angels from wellness/health networks (COREangels Health, Angels Santé), personal network, accelerators
- Milestone: Working prototype, initial user testing, waitlist traction

**Phase 2: Seed ($1.5M–$3M)**
- Instrument: SAFE or priced round
- Sources: Specialist VCs (JAZZ Venture Partners, Wisdom Ventures, Telosity, Able Partners), angel syndicates
- Milestone: Product launch, 5K–10K MAU, retention data proving >10% 30-day retention, early clinical validation

**Phase 3: Supplement with Non-Dilutive Capital**
- EU: EIC Accelerator (€2.5M grant), national programs (Bpifrance, Innovate UK, HTGF)
- US: NSF SBIR Digital Health (if SBIR reauthorized), foundation grants
- Post-revenue: Revenue-based financing (Pipe, Clearco, Lighter Capital) for marketing spend

**Phase 4: Series A ($5M–$15M)**
- Priced equity round
- Sources: Growth-stage health/wellness VCs (General Catalyst, GV, Flare Capital)
- Milestone: 50K+ MAU, clear path to $1M+ ARR, clinical evidence, B2B pipeline
