Applying before 31 December

Five steps, on your own word.

Everything is in the CiRCLE app. Open the certification course and press Apply for certification. Tick each step when it’s done:

1

Watch all the classes, live or as replays.

2

Practise at least 3 times with fellow students, under Practice in the app.

3

View at least 6 Q&A sessions, live or as replays.

4

Reach Level 3 in the Training Quiz.

5

Record a session you lead with a client: 30 minutes at most, on Zoom or in person, with someone who isn’t a student of the course.

Once all five are ticked, Submit for certification opens the application form. Send it before the end of 31 December. Within 90 days I’ll send you personal feedback on your session and, if everything is in place, your certificate.
