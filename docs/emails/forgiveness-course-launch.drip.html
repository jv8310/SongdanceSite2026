There’s a piece of advice that sounds kind and lands heavy: just let it go. Most of us have tried. We said the words, we even meant them — and something in the body didn’t come along.

That’s not a failure of will. What hurt us was felt in the body, and the body rarely changes its mind because the head has made a decision. It wants to be heard first.

Not a sound to make it go away — the sound of what still hurts, acknowledged.

This December, Daniela Hess and I are holding The Forgiveness Course: four live evenings on Zoom where we take forgiveness in its natural order — others, then yourself, then what lies underneath both — with sounding as the way the body takes part.

Each evening runs 19:00–21:00 Brussels time — the course page shows it in your own time. Can’t be there live? Every session is recorded, and the replays are yours to keep.

You don’t need to have forgiven anything, or to feel ready to. You don’t need any experience with sounding. You can stay muted the whole time — soft counts, and a hum is a complete practice.
