There's a piece of advice that sounds kind and lands heavy: just let it go. Most of us have tried. We said the words, we even meant them, and something in the body didn't come along.

That's not a failure of will. What hurt us was felt in the body, and the body rarely changes its mind because the head has made a decision. It wants to be heard first.

Not a sound to make it go away: the sound of what still hurts, acknowledged.
