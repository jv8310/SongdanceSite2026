When Upala and I finished the Authentic Singing Journey, we had nothing left to give it — and no plan for what came next. So we did the only thing that made any sense: we rested.

And we rested by enjoying the very thing we’d made. We put on our own singing journey and danced to it — barefoot, with friends, for no reason but the love of it. Moving because the music asked us to, and for nothing else.

And there, in the middle of all that easy movement — not looking for it, not trying for anything — the next chapter quietly found us.

It became the Magical Movement Journey: ten sessions, each one carried by its own kind of music. And at the very heart of it is the way Upala moved.

She moved the way she sang — never to a script, only ever following what her body was telling her in that moment.
