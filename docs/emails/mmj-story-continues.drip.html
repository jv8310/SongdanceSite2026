After Upala and I finished the Authentic Singing Journey, there was no grand plan for what came next. We’d poured everything into it. So we did the only sensible thing: we rested.

And we rested by enjoying the very thing we’d made. We danced to it — to our own singing journey — in South Africa, with friends, just for the love of it. A lovely, easy time after all that creating.

And somewhere in that flow of moving and dancing, without looking for it, the inspiration for the next chapter found us.

That became the Magical Movement Journey — ten sessions, each carried by a different kind of music. At the heart of it is Upala’s free movement.

She moved the way she sang — never confined to a script, just following whatever her body was telling her in the moment.
