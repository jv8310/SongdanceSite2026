What you learn in those nine months

To travel through the layers of a sound

How to recognise feelings in a voice (high, low, trembling, sharp), how to follow what is underneath the first sound, and how to guide a whole session from beginning to end.

The emotions underneath

Anger, fear, pain and grief. How each one moves, how it disguises itself, and how to meet it through the voice.

Listening with the body

Telling grief from pain, fear from overwhelm. Hearing what the voice is almost saying.

Skills for holding the space

Pacing. Regulation. The volume button. Knowing when to lead, when to hold, and when to let the silence do the work.

 

A note on what it is not: it is not a comprehensive training to become a therapist, coach or psychologist. It is a course in Somatic Vocal Healing specifically. If you mean to work with clients professionally, you should already have a background in holding space.
