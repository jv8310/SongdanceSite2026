WHAT PEOPLE TELL ME BEFORE THE FIRST TIME

- "I can't sing.": Good. This isn't singing. You don't need a good voice, you need your honest one. There is no melody to hit and nobody to please. You cannot do it wrong; it will always express something.
- "Someone will hear me.": Not in the workshop. Your microphone stays off unless you want it on, so the only one who hears your sound is you. And if it is the people at home you are thinking of: that is the most common question in this work. Some people start in their car or their bathroom. Soft counts.
- "What if nothing comes?": Then that is what's there. A closed door has a sound too, and we start with that one.

Nobody will ask you to scream, and nobody is put on the spot. Nothing here needs you to be ready.
