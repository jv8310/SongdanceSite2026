# "Education, not therapy": audit

*18 November 2026. Phase 1 of the brief: a report. Nothing on the site or in the app has been
changed. Companion files in this folder: `intake_proposal.md` and `safety_page_draft.md`.*

Read for this audit: every page, component and email in the site repository that a visitor or
buyer can see, the app's strings in five languages, its emails and store listings, and the live
course texts in the app for the 12-week course (English and German), the certification course,
Grief, Forgiveness and Inner Child. Each quotation below is word for word from the file and line
given. What could not be read is listed under "Not audited".

## What the findings come down to

**1. The live sessions are described as the place to bring what you carry.** This is the
clearest source of the wrong expectation. The day-1 email of both the 12-week and the
certification series explains the Q&As like this: *"You may bring strong emotions, subtle
sensations, life situations … or simply a wish to listen and be held by the group field"*, with
*"real-time vocal processes"* and *"deep guided journeys"* (A1, A2). The certification page:
*"Where students bring what came up in a practice session and want to look at it together"*
(A3). The live pass: *"whatever came up in your practice"* (A4). The deepening sessions:
*"experiences that need more space"* (A5), and in the app *"what the last one opened, what to
bring to the next"* (D2). The Q&A email is Drip's own text with two words changed, so Drip is
probably sending the original today.

**2. Practice Together is worded as receiving a session.** *"To give and receive"* in eight places
on the site and three emails (A8 to A13, B44); *"To receive a session."* in the app in all five
languages (D6); *"12-week students can receive one"* in the App Store and Play listings (D7).
*"You certify by sounding, by holding space, by being held."* (A10)

**3. The certification course is sold as a personal journey.** *"Many participants are here to go
further into their own healing"* (A6). *"This journey begins with your own voice and your own
healing."* (A7, the path's welcome email.) *"What begins as professional training becomes a
deeply personal journey"* and *"You can come out a certified practitioner, or just changed."*
(B1, B2, sent to every masterclass attendee.) *"…or simply go fully in with your own voice"*
(B37 on the site, E14 in the app).

**4. Safety is promised.** The copy book says "seldom is not never"; these say never. *"the reason
it stays safe: the voice self-titrates, never opening more than a person can be with"* (B6, to
professionals, about a client in overwhelm). *"the voice never opens more than you can be
with"* (B27). *"make it safe enough that what once felt unbearable can finally be heard — and
finished"* (A14, the 12-week page). *"the five core wounds met safely"* (B3, and E8, E19 in the
app). *"the voice only brings up what you can be with"* (C30: Forgiveness email #21, due
3 December, not yet sent). The retreat intake's own invitation opens *"To safeguard a held and
grounded container"* (C29).

**5. Facilitators are given care-profession titles.** Lorenz is *"Somatischer Therapeut"* on both
German pages (A17, B4). Jacob *"has guided thousands of students worldwide through deep inner
healing … serving clients globally"* (C4). Daniela's *"decades of counselling grief"* (B50, C32)
beside "grief educators" elsewhere. Karen Evans is "Dr." and "Chiropraktikerin" in one place,
"a former chiropractor" in another, and "came to the work as a client, now co-facilitator" in a
third (A28).

**6. The Inner Child journey promises the most and says the least.** The page offers *"optional
1:1 support"* three times and the welcome email repeats it (C1, C2); nothing says what that is.
The lesson texts in the app: *"so that you no longer have to carry it"*, *"so that it can be
released"*, *"these wounds can be healed"*, *"you and your inner child have healed so much"*
(E1 to E5). It is a €29 recording about childhood pain, with nobody present, and it has no
"not therapy" line anywhere.

**7. The heaviest situations are invited in with no boundary beside them.** The Forgiveness FAQ
on abuse and assault: *"You're welcome as you are … We go at the pace your body allows"* (C6),
for a live group on Zoom. The Grief page: *"we guide you through an intentional process … with
someone who knows the way"* (C3), for four recordings. Heal the Healer names *"compassion
fatigue, secondary stress, burnout"* and answers *"Somatic Vocal Healing meets it at the level
where it lives"* (B5).

**8. Some testimonials are health claims.** Karen Evans on the certification page: her own
recovery, *"old, severe emotional traumas"*, clients *"in therapy for 20 years"* now going to
the opera (part A). A migraine *"completely gone"* by the end of a session (part A). "Dr. Karen"
on three sales pages: *"I've never met a method that works as quickly, deeply, gently, and
effectively"* (part B). In German: *"Der Kurs ermöglicht schnelle, tiefe Heilung"* and *"weit
über klassische Heilmethoden hinaus"* (part A). Rebecca on Ritual of Belonging: *"a container in
which we could truly process our lives"* (part C). Two of these are from your own co-facilitator.

**9. The statement exists, but where nobody reads it.** See the table below. The most complete
"this is not therapy" is on the private sessions page. The app says it nowhere; its note to
Apple's reviewers says the app *"says plainly that it is not therapy"* (part D).

**10. Old pages are still live.** The German 12-week page shows a start date of 10 May 2026 and
an early-bird discount, and carries four of the High findings. The Dutch Klankopstellingen page
shows June 2026 dates and has no statement. Three draft workshop pages (`/workshop-new`,
`/workshop-new2`, `/workshop-parallax`) are reachable, take payments and carry the oldest copy.
The German pages are translations of earlier English versions and have none of the corrections
the English pages gained since.

## Decisions for Jacob

These come before the rewrites, because the rewrites depend on them.

1. **The canonical statement.** Your message says healing happens naturally through the learning;
   the brief's draft avoids the word. `safety_page_draft.md` says it once, after the learning.
2. **One pair of words.** The brief: "education, not therapy" and "space holders, not healers".
   The certification course's last class: "Healing, not therapy". The copy book: a therapeutic
   framework on top of somatic sounding. Suggested: the *course* is education; a practitioner
   *holds space*; healing is the participant's own.
3. **Practice Together.** Whether "give" and "receive" stay as the names of the two roles (they
   are built into the app), and what the sentence around them says.
4. **Class and method names**: "Therapeutic Foundations", "Therapeutic skills", "Healing
   Templates", "The ABC of Healing", "A Healing Journey", and "client" throughout the
   certification material. All are marked "method description: not to be rewritten without your
   approval".
5. **Product and campaign names**: "Inner Child Healing Journey" (the menu already says "Inner
   Child Journey"), "Heal the Healer", and "Vocal Healing Workshop", where dropping "Somatic"
   makes "healing" describe the workshop.
6. **"Trauma-informed"** on the workshop and masterclass pages, and "traumainformierte
   Heilungsarbeit" on the German one.
7. **Whether the Inner Child 1:1 support exists.** If it is the private session, it should say so.
8. **The testimonials.** 26 are listed; 7 are recommended for removal and 4 for trimming at a
   sentence end. And whether a co-facilitator's words belong among participants' at all.
9. **Titles**: Lorenz, Daniela and Karen Evans, each to be confirmed with the person.
10. **The stale pages**: German 12-week, Klankopstellingen, the three workshop drafts.
11. **What a certificate means.** Nothing on the certification page, in the "you are certified"
    email or on the certificate says a graduate is a space holder and not a therapist.
12. **Workshop and masterclass**: an acknowledgment box, or a visible line above the pay button.
    These forms have no terms box today either.
13. **Two house lines** from the copy book that state a result as a rule: "acknowledged things
    leave on their own" (four places) and "Talking circles it. Sounding enters it." on the page
    for therapists.
14. **Private sessions**: whether course pages and the Inner Child page may point to them for
    people whose main need is personal support (the brief's own open question).
15. **The 12-week course**: the same wording as the certification course, or its own, since it
    is closer to personal work by design (also the brief's).

## How to read the parts

Each part has the same five sections: findings (High first, then Medium, then Low as a short
list), testimonials to decide, where the statement is and is missing, other languages, and open
questions. Finding numbers carry the part's letter (A1, B12). A rewrite marked "method
description" is not proposed for change without your approval. No participant's words are
reworded anywhere: a testimonial is kept, trimmed at a sentence end, or removed.
